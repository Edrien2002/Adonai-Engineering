Built on precision, run on trust.

We started as a materials dealer and grew into a full delivery partner because clients kept asking us to see the job through past the delivery note.

One dealer, then one contractor, now one project partner

Adonai Engineering Services began by supplying construction and architectural materials to builders and homeowners around Uganda. Being on-site so often, the same question kept coming up: could we also handle the design, the surveying, or the trades instead of the client coordinating five separate contractors themselves?

That's the business we built. Today a client can bring us a plot and a rough idea, and leave with a surveyed site, an architectural drawing, sourced materials and a building crew that includes plumbing and electrical, coordinated as one job instead of five invoices.

We keep the team small enough that the person who scoped your project is the person who checks in on it at site.
